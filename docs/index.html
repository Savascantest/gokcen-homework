01

Grammar refresher

Past Simple tells us about finished actions: She called yesterday. They went home. Use didn’t + base verb for negatives: I didn’t go.

Time prepositions: at 7:30 / the station · on Tuesday / 5 October · in the morning / 2026 / Berlin.

03

Reading · A changed plan

Lena planned every detail of her trip to the coastal town. She booked the overnight train because it fitted around her work schedule, packed one small bag, and saved the hotel address on her phone. On Friday evening, she arrived at the station early. The platform was busy, but she found her compartment and read a novel until the train left. During the journey, she slept badly because a child in the next compartment was crying. Still, she woke up excited when she saw the sea through the window.

At the destination, Lena discovered that her phone battery was almost empty. She could not check the map or call the hotel. For a few minutes, she felt annoyed with herself. Then a passenger from the train, Farid, noticed her looking at the street signs. He offered his portable charger and explained which bus she needed. On the bus, they talked about travel mistakes and laughed about their worst journeys. Lena reached the hotel later than planned, but she was no longer worried. The next day, she explored the town on foot, tried a small bakery near the harbour, and sent Farid a message to say thank you. The trip was not perfect, but it taught her that a changed plan can lead to a good conversation.
